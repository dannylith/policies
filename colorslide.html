Privacy Policy for Color Slide

Last updated: 30 September 2026

Color Slide is a block puzzle game for iPhone. This policy explains what information is collected when you play it, who collects it, and what choices you have.

In short: the game itself does not collect or transmit any personal information about you. It has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics. Data collection comes only from Google's advertising service and from the service that verifies in-app purchases, both described below.

Information the app collects

None. Color Slide does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. There is no account to create and nothing to sign in to.

Your game progress is saved on your device only, and never sent anywhere by the game:

  • Your level, coins, lives, boosters, themes, and daily reward streak
  • Your sound, music, and haptics settings
  • Which in-app purchases you have made

All of it is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising

The game displays advertisements supplied by Google AdMob: a banner during play, occasional full-screen ads between levels, and optional reward videos that only play when you tap to watch one. Google is an independent company and its collection of data is governed by its own privacy policy, not this one.

To serve and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • Your device's advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking
  • Your IP address, and an approximate location derived from it
  • Device information such as model and operating system version
  • Information about how you interact with ads that are shown

You can read how Google uses this information at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services, and Google's own privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices about advertising

  • Tracking permission. The first time you open Color Slide, iOS asks whether the app may track your activity across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Consent (EU, UK, and Switzerland). If you are in a region where consent is required, you will be shown a consent form before any personalised advertising data is collected. You can reopen it at any time from Color Slide → Settings → Ad Privacy Choices.
  • Remove ads. The "Remove Ads" or "Starter Pack" purchase removes the banner and the full-screen ads between levels. Optional reward videos stay available, and only play if you choose to watch one.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Apple. Payment is handled through your Apple Account, and the developer of this game never receives or has access to your payment card details, billing address, or Apple Account credentials.

To confirm purchases and restore them on your devices, the game uses RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous user ID created for the app, the details of your purchases, and basic device information such as operating system version and App Store country. It does not receive your name, email address, or payment details. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Children

Color Slide is not directed to children under the age of 13, and does not knowingly collect information from children. The advertising in the game is not configured as child-directed.

Data sharing and sale

The developer of Color Slide does not sell, rent, or share any personal information, because none is collected. Advertising data is handled by Google, and purchase data by Apple and RevenueCat, as described above.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, or California), you may have rights to access or delete data held by the services above. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device. For anything else, contact us and we will help you make the request.

Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects privacy (for example if analytics or an additional service is added), this policy will be updated and the date at the top of this page will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to dannypolicy@outlook.com.
